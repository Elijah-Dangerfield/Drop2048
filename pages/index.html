Doublestack

Like if 2048 and Tetris had a baby.

Doublestack is THE 2048 game with tiles that drop. Slide each falling tile into place and land it on a matching number to combine them. Chain those combos together and keep going until you hit 2048. The tiles fall faster the longer you last, and you'll run into a bump or two along the way.

Plays offline. No account, no signup, nothing to sign in to.
